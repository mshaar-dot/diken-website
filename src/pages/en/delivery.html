The fleet

Why we own 80 vehicles.

Most fleets rent capacity from whoever has a motorcycle. Diken bought and operates 80 of its own so that service to the client does not depend on who turned up, and so that someone with no vehicle can still make delivery a full-time job.

Cash is the other discipline. Over JOD 30 million a year is collected on delivery, reconciled daily with the help of the operations agent described on the Technology page.
