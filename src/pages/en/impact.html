Impact

Work that finds you. Income that grows with you.

Delivery is the largest door into work in Jordan. Diken has spent a decade making that door wider.

Social responsibility, as firsts.

1st

Captains with disabilities

Diken was the first company in Jordan to onboard riders with disabilities. With Talabat's support it introduced special working conditions: routes, hours and equipment adapted so the work is safe and the income steady.

1st

Women captains

First to onboard female riders in Jordan's delivery sector, with the same pay and the same support, and working conditions developed with Talabat.

1st

Zero down payment, zero interest

With Microfund for Women, a financing product for people who have no vehicle and no job. The motorcycle is financed at zero down payment and zero interest, and the job starts the same week.

With the Ministry of Labour

Social security for full-time captains

Delivery becomes a job with a record. Captains who want full-time income are registered for social security.

80 vehicles

A fleet for people without one

Diken bought and operates 80 vehicles so that not owning a motorcycle is no longer a reason to stay out of work.

1st

Green energy, on the road.

Through Areena, Diken brought the first electric scooter with battery swapping to Jordan, with a Talabat-sponsored incentive for captains who switch. Swapping removes the two reasons riders resisted electric: range and waiting.
