Scope

This policy covers personal information handled by Diken Bros (شركة الدكن) when you visit dikendelivery.com, send us a message through the site, or contact us by phone or email using the details published here. It does not cover the platforms you may use as a captain (Talabat, Careem, HungerStation, noon), which have their own privacy policies, or the separate agreements you sign at a branch.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you use the contact form, your mail app sends us the fields you filled in: name, company, email address, phone number, the topic you chose and your message. When you call or email us, we receive the details you share and, for calls, the number you called from.
  • Captain applications. If you enquire about becoming a captain, we may ask about your licence, vehicle, city and availability so a branch can follow up. Any documents are collected at the branch, not through this site.
  • Technical data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested for security and operation of the service. The site itself does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • to answer your enquiry and route it to the right division or branch;
  • to follow up a partnership, agency, fleet, sales, investment or press request;
  • to contact you about a captain application and invite you to a branch;
  • to keep records of our correspondence and comply with legal obligations;
  • to protect the site and our business against fraud and misuse.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Legal basis and consent

We process your information because you asked us to (to respond to your message), because it is needed to take steps towards an agreement with you, because we have a legitimate interest in running and protecting our business, or because the law requires it. Where consent is required, sending the form or contacting us is how you give it, and you may withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

Who we share it with

  • Inside the group. The Diken division or branch best placed to answer you.
  • Partners, only when needed. If your request concerns a platform, a financing partner such as Microfund for Women, or a brand we represent, we may share the details needed to progress it, and we will tell you when we do.
  • Service providers. Email and hosting providers that process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.
  • Authorities. When required by law, a court order, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards, normally no more than two years, unless a longer period is required by law or by an agreement we enter into with you. Captain application details that do not lead to onboarding are deleted within twelve months.

Cookies and third-party resources

The site does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. To display correctly it loads the Alexandria typeface from Google Fonts and the Phosphor icon set from the jsDelivr content network; those providers may see your IP address when your browser fetches the files, and process it under their own privacy policies.

Where it is stored

The site is served from GitHub Pages, whose servers may be located outside Jordan. Messages you send reach our email systems, which may also be hosted outside Jordan. By contacting us you acknowledge that your information may be processed in those locations, protected by the providers' contractual and security commitments.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it for a particular purpose. Write to info@dikenbros.com with enough detail for us to identify you and your request; we will answer within thirty days. You may also complain to the competent authority in Jordan if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully.

Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen. Captains must meet the legal age and licensing requirements in Jordan.

Security

We limit access to correspondence to the people who need it to answer you, and we use providers that protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; please do not send us sensitive information such as identification numbers or bank details through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: info@dikenbros.com, 06 416 6660, or in writing to Diken Bros, Amman, Jordan.
