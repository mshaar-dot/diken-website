About

A family group that kept finding the next job to do.

Diken Bros started in 1990 with spare parts. Every division since came from a customer's problem nobody else would solve.

The story, in order.

1990

Founded in Amman

Spare-parts supply and distribution for multinational companies operating in Jordan.

2019

Delivery begins

A delivery company built to serve Talabat and Careem.

Growth

Riyadh and Dubai, built and sold

Diken built delivery operations in Saudi Arabia and the UAE from scratch and sold both as going concerns.

Agencies

Hero, SYM, Motul, Wabco, BPW

Exclusive agency agreements for Jordan, with the group's own fleet as the first customer.

Investments

Samhouri & Diken, Areena

Half of a leading insulation supplier, a quarter of the first battery-swap scooter network.

Firsts

Women, disabilities, financing

The first women captains and captains with disabilities in Jordan's delivery sector, and zero-down financing with Microfund for Women.

Green

The swap scooter

Jordan's first electric scooter with battery swapping, with a Talabat-sponsored incentive.

Today

An AI-run operation

5,000+ captains, 20,000 orders a day, and agents on dispatch, cash, partner orders and reporting.

What we believe.

At Diken, work looks for you, and no problem goes unsolved.

عند الدكن، الشغل بدور عليك، وما في مشكلة ما إلها حل.

Partners are peers
