25% · Areena

Green scooter solutions and Jordan's first swap grid

Areena is the exclusive agent for Blue Shark electric scooters. With Talabat and Total it set up the first battery-swapping network for scooters in Jordan.

A swap takes a minute. Captains stop waiting for a charge, ride further, and spend a fraction of what fuel costs.

Why swapping matters.

A scooter battery covers a short day. A swap station turns that into an unlimited one.

RangeThe limit of a single battery disappears when a fresh one is a stop away.
CostElectricity instead of fuel, and the Talabat-sponsored incentive on top.
TimeMinutes at a cabinet, not hours at a charger.
