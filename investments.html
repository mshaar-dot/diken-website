Two companies we helped build.

Diken invests where it can add distribution, operations or a fleet of customers. Two holdings today.

Portfolio

Two holdings.

50% · Samhouri & Diken

A leading insulation and duct-accessories supplier

Samhouri & Diken represents Izocam, SABIC Steel, Vents and other brands in Jordan and supplies the country's largest construction projects.

Projects supplied: Le Royal Hotel, Sheraton Amman, City Mall, and Queen Alia International Airport.

25% · Areena

Green scooter solutions and Jordan's first swap grid

Areena is the exclusive agent for Blue Shark electric scooters. With Talabat and Total it set up the first battery-swapping network for scooters in Jordan.

A swap takes a minute. Captains stop waiting for a charge, ride further, and spend a fraction of what fuel costs.
